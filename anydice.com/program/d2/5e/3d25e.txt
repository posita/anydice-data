\ input a different die in the DAMAGE variable to generate different Average Balanced MP Costs for Reaper's Touch, as well as the damage and healing it would do \

DAMAGE: 2d10


HEALING: DAMAGE/2
D_MP: 2*DAMAGE/3
H_MP: 3*HEALING/2

output (D_MP+H_MP)*4 named "Balanced MP Cost"
output ((D_MP+H_MP)*4*8)/10 named "Balanced MP Cost w/ Focus"
output ((D_MP+H_MP)*4*6)/10 named "Balanced MP Cost w/ Concentration"

output DAMAGE named "Damage Per Target per round"
output HEALING*3 named "Healing Per Round w/ 3 targets"
