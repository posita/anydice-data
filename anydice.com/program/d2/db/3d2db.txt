\ THUNDERSTORM spell Balance Calculator \

E_DD: 2d10                            \Electric Damage Dice\
S_DD: 1d4                             \Sonic Damage Dice\
B: 1                                  \Number of Bolts\

E_DA: (E_DD*62/100)+((E_DD/2)*38/100) \Electric Damage Accounting for Accuracy (62% hit chance)\
E_DE: E_DA*10/10                      \Electric damage for an estimated 1 affected target on average per bolt\

SC: ((2^((2/(1/(2*B)))))+16)

S_DA: (S_DD*62/100)+((S_DD/2)*38/100) \Sonic Damage Accounting for Accuracy (62% hit chance)\
S_DE: S_DA*SC/10                      \Sonic damage on average for an estimated number of affected targets/bolt(the number that gets divided by 10? that/10 is #targets/bolt). Scale this number down as the number of bolts increases, with 3 being 1 bolt, 2 being 3 bolts, 1 being 6 bolts, etc. (never to 0)\

\T_DE: (E_DE+S_DE)*B   \                \Total damage estimate\

\MPC: T_DE*2/3  \                       \Balanced Mana Point Cost\

output SC

\output E_DA+S_DA named "Single target direct hit damage"
output E_DA*4+S_DA named "Single target focus fire damage"\
\output T_DE named "Total average damage estimate"\
\output MPC named "Balanced Mana Point Cost"\
