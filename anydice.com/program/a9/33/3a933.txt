\ At Least So Many Of At Least Such A Value \
\ e.g. what are the odds of 3 or more dice showing a 4 or greater when rolling 6 dice? \

SIDES: 6 \ number of sides on the dice; e.g. 6 for a 6-sided dice \
DICE:  6 \ number of dice to roll \
VALUE: 4 \ minimum value displayed on the dice \
COUNT: 3 \ minimum quantity of dice displaying said value \

output DICEd(dSIDES >= VALUE) >= COUNT named "odds of [COUNT] or more dice showing [VALUE] or greater when rolling [DICE] [SIDES]-sided dice"
