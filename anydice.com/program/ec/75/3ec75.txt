function: compare ATTACKER_DIE_A ATTACKER_DIE_B vs DEFENDER_DIE_A DEFENDER_DIE_B {

AT: ATTACKER_DIE_A > ATTACKER_DIE_B
DE: DEFENDER_DIE_A > DEFENDER_DIE_B

AA: ATTACKER_DIE_A > DEFENDER_DIE_A
BB: ATTACKER_DIE_B > DEFENDER_DIE_B
AB: ATTACKER_DIE_A > DEFENDER_DIE_B
BA: ATTACKER_DIE_B > DEFENDER_DIE_A

X: AT&DE&AA|!AT&DE&BA|AT&!DE&AB|!AT&!DE&BB
Y: AT&DE&BB|!AT&DE&AB|AT&!DE&BA|!AT&!DE&AA

result: 2*X + Y
}

loop D over {4,6,8,10,12}{
ATT_A: d4
ATT_B: d10

DEF_A: d6
DEF_B: d8

output [compare ATT_A ATT_B vs DEF_A DEF_B]
}
