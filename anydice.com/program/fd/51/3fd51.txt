\Trinity Fall Damage Calculator\

H: 15           \Distance fallen, in meters\
W: 0            \Into liquid? 1=True, 0=False\
CONT: 0         \Controlled Fall? 1=True, 0=False\
DEX: 3          \Dexterity Modifier. Use 0 for Objects\
BONUS: 6        \Acrobatics Skill Bonus, for Cat Fall\
RES: 3          \Resistance to Bludgeoning and Fall damage\





A:(Hd4-RES-CONT*(DEXd4))/(1+W)
B: A-(3d12+DEX+BONUS)

output A named "Flat Damage"
output B named "Cat Fall"
