function: ROLL:s target X:n auto Y:n { \ROLL is the roll on all autofire attacks, whether that's one attack with a single gun or dual-wielding; ROLL is a sequence of each attack roll made (I'm ignoring crits and fumbles for simplicity and because they won't meaningfully affect the spread); X is the DV minus the BASE (bc it makes life easier); Y is the autofire rating\
  Z: 0 \Z is the total damage dealt\
  loop W over ROLL { \this block will loop through a number of times equal to the number of of attack rolls, however W, instead of storing the loop count, will store whichever attack roll we're looking at in that moment\
    if (W>X) {
      Z: Z + (2d6 * [lowest of (W-X) and Y]) \this will calculate the damage for an individual attack roll\
    }
  }
  result: Z \Z contains the total damage, so we can just output it\
}
output [1d10 target 4 auto 3] named "a single SMG at BASE 9 against DV 13"
output [2d10 target 7 auto 3] named "dual-wielded SMGs with BASE 9 at -3 against DV 13"
