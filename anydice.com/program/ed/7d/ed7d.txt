 that the actual probability is 1-in-1024 per share. 1024 divided by 16 = 64. Thus a 16-in-1024
 chance is the same as a 1-in-64 chance.

 By default, this shows the odds of getting exactly that many gems. Obviously, you can't get more
 than one gem with only one spin. A, perhaps, more useful comparison is gotten by clicking
 "At Least" below. Explained in the forum post.
