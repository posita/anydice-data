\PF1E Battle Calculator\

\Variable Definitions (Put your raw data here)\

MONSTER_AC: 16
MONSTER_HP: 4
MONSTER_DR: 0 \Use a 0 if an attack bypasses the DR or a negative number if there is vulnerability\

ATTACK_BONUS: 5
DAMAGE: 1d8+3
CRIT_DAMAGE: 2d8+6
CRIT_THREAT: 20

\Math(do not change this unless you know what you're doing)\

ATTACK: 1d20 + ATTACK_BONUS
HIT_CHANCE: (ATTACK >= MONSTER_AC) | (1d20 = 20 & MONSTER_AC > ATTACK_BONUS+20)
DAMAGE: DAMAGE - MONSTER_DR
CRIT_DAMAGE: CRIT_DAMAGE - MONSTER_DR
CRIT_CHANCE: HIT_CHANCE & 1d20 >= CRIT_THREAT

\Outputs\

output HIT_CHANCE named "Chance to Hit"
output DAMAGE named "Damage Range"
output DAMAGE > 0 named "Chance to Deal Damage On Hit"
output DAMAGE >= MONSTER_HP named "Chance for One Hit Kill On Non-Critical Hit"

output CRIT_CHANCE named "Chance to Critical Hit"
output CRIT_DAMAGE named "Critical Damage Range"
output CRIT_DAMAGE > 0 named "Chance to Deal Damage On Critical Hit"
output CRIT_DAMAGE >= MONSTER_HP named "Chance for One Hit Kill On Critical Hit"

output ((HIT_CHANCE & !CRIT_CHANCE) & DAMAGE > 0) | (CRIT_CHANCE & CRIT_DAMAGE > 0) named "Chance to Deal Damage At All"
output ((HIT_CHANCE & !CRIT_CHANCE) & DAMAGE >= MONSTER_HP) | (CRIT_CHANCE & CRIT_DAMAGE >= MONSTER_HP) named "Chance for One Hit Kill Happening"
