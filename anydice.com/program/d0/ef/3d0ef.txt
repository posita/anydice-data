ONE: 1d12
TWO: 1d12
THREE: 1d8

FOUR: 1d8
FIVE: 1d10
SIX: 1d10


function: middle of A:n and B:n and C:n {
SUM: A+B+C
SUM: SUM-[lowest of A and [lowest of B and C]]
result: SUM-[highest of A and [highest of B and C]]
}

ATTACK: [middle of ONE and TWO and THREE]
DEFENSE: [middle of FOUR and FIVE and SIX]
output [highest of 0 and (ATTACK - DEFENSE)]

ATTACKTWO: [middle of ONE and TWO and THREE] + [lowest of ONE and [lowest of TWO and THREE]]
DEFENSETWO: [middle of FOUR and FIVE and SIX] + [lowest of FOUR and [lowest of FIVE and SIX]]
output [highest of 0 and (ATTACKTWO - DEFENSETWO)]
