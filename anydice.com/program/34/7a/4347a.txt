\My best attempt at a Forbidden Lands setup.                                         \
\To use it, in place of 3d6, or 1d12, you would use one of the representations below.\
\d6  - {0,0,0,0,0,1}                                                                 \
\d8  - {{0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2}                                                            \
\d10 - {0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3}                                                         \
\d12 - {0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3,4}                                                     \
\For example, 4d6 + 1d12 would be written as:                                        \
\4d{0,0,0,0,0,1} + 1d{0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3,4}                                       \
\------------------------------------------------------------------------------------\

output 1d{0,0,0,0,0,1} + 1d{0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2} + 1d{0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3} + 1d{0,0,0,0,0,1,1,2,2,3,3,4} named "1d6+1d8+1d10+1d12"
