function: attack D:n MOD:n PROF:n BONUS:n WEAPONDICE:n{
result:(MOD+PROF+BONUS+D>=AC)*(WEAPONDICE)\*((D/20)+1)\
}

T:0
loop I over {1..ATTACKS}{
T:T + [attack 1@(ADV+1)d20 MOD PROF BONUS  WEAPONDICE]
}
result: T
}
