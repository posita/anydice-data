function: roll D:s comp C:n  seuil S:n chance L:n{
A:[count {S..6} in D]
B:[count {S-1} in D]
E:[count {S-2} in D]
F:[lowest of B and C]
G:[highest of (C-F)/2 and 0]
H:[lowest of G and E]
X:A+F+H
if L=0{result:X}
else{
H:#D-A-E-G
result:X+[count {4..6} in [lowest of H and L]d6]}}

output [roll 5d6 comp 2 seuil 4 chance 0]
output [roll 5d6 comp 0 seuil 4 chance 3]
