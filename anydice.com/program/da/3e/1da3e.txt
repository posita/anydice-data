\stat input\
\str is your modifere that is added to both attack and damage\
STR:3
\prof is your proficency, and is added only to the attack\
PROF:2
\misc is other things added to both damage and attack\
MISC:0
\wepdie is what die/dice your weapon use\
WEPDIE:3d6
\if you have disadvantage use a negativ number, 0 for nothing and a positive number for advantage\
ADVANTAGE:2
\other attack values\
OAT:0
\other damage values\
ODA:2

\calcs som variables based on input\
ATTACK:STR+PROF+MISC+OAT
DAMAGE:STR+MISC+WEPDIE+ODA
GWMVALUE: {-5,10}


\helper function to figur out what your attack die is based on advantage\
function: advantage of ADV {
if ADV > 0 {D: [highest 1 of 2d20]}
if ADV < 0 {D: [lowest 1 of 2d20]}
if ADV = 0 {D: 1d20}
result: D
}
\variable based calling the helper function\
DIE: [advantage of ADVANTAGE]  
\loop that roles an attack against any AC from 10 to 20\
loop AC over {10..20} {
  output (DIE+ATTACK >=AC)* (DAMAGE) named "without gwm vs [AC]"
  output (DIE+ATTACK+1@GWMVALUE >=AC)* (DAMAGE+2@GWMVALUE) named "with gwm vs [AC]" 
}
