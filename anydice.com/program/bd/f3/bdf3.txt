function: lance D:s comp C:n risque R:n{
EB:[count {2} in D]
EA:[count {3} in D]
SA:[count {4..5} in D]
SB:[count {6} in D]
SO:SA+SB
if SO=0{result:0} 
else {
SC:[lowest of C and EA]
S:SC+SO
CA:C-SC

if R>=0{SD:[count {4..6} in [lowest of CA and SB]d6] CB:[highest of CA-SB and 0] SE:[lowest of 2*CB and EB]/2 result:S+SD+SE}
else{SE:[lowest of 2*CA and EB]/2 CB:[highest of CA-2*SE and 0] SD:[count {4..6} in [lowest of CB and SB]d6] result:S+SD+SE}}}

output[lance 4d6 comp 0 risque 0]
output[lance 4d6 comp 3 risque -1]
output[lance 4d6 comp 3 risque 1]
