function: lance D:s comp C:n risque R:n{
EB:[count {2} in D]
EA:[count {3} in D]
SA:[count {4..5} in D]
SB:[count {6} in D]
SC:[lowest of C and EA]
S:SC+SA+SB
CA:C-SC
if R>0{SD:[count {4..6} in [lowest of CA and SB]d6]
CB:[highest of CA-SB and 0]
SE:[lowest of 2*CB and EB]/2
result:S+SD+SE}
else{
SE:[lowest of 2*CA and EB]/2
CB:[highest of CA-2*SE and 0]
SD:[count {4..6} in [lowest of CB and SB]d6]
result:S+SD+SE}}

output[lance 8d6 comp 0 risque 0]
output[lance 8d6 comp 2 risque 0]
output[lance 8d6 comp 2 risque 1]
