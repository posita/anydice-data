\Chance for atropa/datura darts to effect enemy, assuming darts hit and enemy isn't immune\
\0 is the chance to fail, 1 is the chance to succeed\
\success chance rounded down matches in-game info\

\Set your stealth and throwing here\
STEALTH: 27
THROWING: 15


function: vs HD:n {
  result: (HD<15 & d100<=3)                       \free 3% chance roll if HD is under 15\
    | d(2*(THROWING + STEALTH)/3 + 4) + 2 > HD    \otherwise roll against HD\
}

\examples are just what I thought of at the time, not necessarily good targets\
output [vs 4] named "bullfrog"
output [vs 7] named "yak"
output [vs 13] named "hydra"
output [vs 20] named "killer klown"
