\The numbers in parenthesis to the right of the table name are the average outcome and standard deviation\
\So for "Number of Skulls Rolling 6 Dice" the average outcome is 1 skull with a std dev of 0.91\
\The "At Least" data format is another good way of checking "how often do I roll at least 2 skulls" for example\

SKULLDIE: d{0,0,0,0,0,1}

loop I over {4,6,8,10,12} {
  output I d SKULLDIE named "Number of Skulls Rolling [I] Dice"
}
