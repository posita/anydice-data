function: roll R:n comp C:n  seuil S:n chance L:n{
if R<8-C{O:C
Y:0}
else{O:C-1
Y:1}
D:(R+O+L)d6
A:[count {S..6} in D]
B:[count {S-1} in D]
E:[count {S-2} in D]
F:[lowest of B and C]
G:[highest of (C-F)/2 and 0]
H:[lowest of G and E]
I:[highest of 0 and C-F-H]
K:[count {6} in D]
X:A+F+H+[roll [lowest of K and (I+L)] comp 0 seuil S chance 0]+Y
result:X}


output[roll 10 comp 5 seuil 4 chance 0]
output[roll 10 comp 6 seuil 4 chance 0]
output[roll 10 comp 5 seuil 4 chance 1]
