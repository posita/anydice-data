function: roll D:s comp C:n  seuil S:n {
A:[count {S..6} in D]
B:[count {S-1} in D]
E:[count {S-2} in D]
F:[lowest of B and C]
G:[highest of (C-F)/2 and 0]
H:[lowest of G and E]
I:[highest of 0 and C-F-H]
K:[count {6} in D]
X:A+F+H+[count {4..6} in [lowest of K and I]d6]
result:X}

output [roll 10d6 comp 0 seuil 4]-[roll 11d6 comp 1 seuil 4]
