function: highest out of DFOUR:s and DSIX:s and DEIGHT:s and DTEN:s and DTWELVE:s{
        \ Order the highest (1@) and second highest (2@) result... \
        \ ...of all the separate dice pools into a list. \
  POT: {1@DFOUR,2@DFOUR,1@DSIX,2@DSIX,1@DEIGHT,2@DEIGHT,1@DTEN,2@DTEN,1@DTWELVE,2@DTWELVE}
        \ Sorts the list and returns the two highest results ({1..2}) from that list. \
  result: {1}@[sort POT]
}

output 2d4
output [highest out of 0d4 and 1d6 and 0d8 and 0d10 and 0d12] + 1d4
output [highest out of 0d4 and 2d6 and 0d8 and 0d10 and 0d12] + 1d4
output [highest out of 0d4 and 0d6 and 0d8 and 0d10 and 1d12] + 1d4
