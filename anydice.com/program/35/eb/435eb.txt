\Check whether there are more results of N in action dice than danger dice\
function: check N:n AA:s BB:s {
if (N = AA) > (N=BB){result: N}
else {result: 0}
}

\Number of dice\
ACTION:4
DANGER:6

\Roll the dice\
AR: ACTIONd6
DR: DANGERd6

\Check what numbers are not cancelled by danger dice and put them on a sequence\
NEW: {}
loop N over {1..6}{
NEW: {NEW, [check N AR DR]}
}

output NEW named "Final dice"
output [highest 1 of NEW] named "result"
