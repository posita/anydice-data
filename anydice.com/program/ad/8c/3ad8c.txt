Recovery: The basic mechanic when you use a recovery is always the same. You regain lost hit points by rolling recovery dice equal to your level and adding your Constitution  modifier. (At 7th level you get to roll more recovery dice, see your class progression table.) Your class indicates which recovery die you will use (usually a d6, d8, or d10).

At 5th level, you double the bonus you get from your Con modifier.
At 8th level, you quadruple the Con modifier and add the epic-tier adjustment: +10 per epic level.

Strong Recovery (1e)
Adventurer Tier: When you roll recovery dice, reroll one of the dice and use the higher result. At 5th level, reroll two of the dice. At 8th level, reroll three.

Strong Recovery (2e)
Adventurer Tier: When you roll recovery dice, add +3 to the result. At 5th level, add +6 instead. At 8th level, add +12 instead.
