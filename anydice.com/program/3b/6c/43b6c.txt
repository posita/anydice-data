\ The function takes two separate pools and counts the successes across both \
function: count successes in POOLA:s and POOLB:s target TARGET:n {
  SUCCESSES: [count {TARGET..20} in POOLA] + [count {TARGET..20} in POOLB]
  result: SUCCESSES
}
