function: highest N:n of A:s and B:s {
  result: {1..N} @ [sort {A, B}]
}

function: lowest N:n of A:s and B:s {
  LEN: #A + #B
  result: {LEN-N+1 .. LEN} @ [sort {A, B}]
}
