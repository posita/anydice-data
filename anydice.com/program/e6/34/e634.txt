function: M at least N {
  result: M >= N
}
SPEED: 2 \your weapon speed + nat speed\
ACCURACY: 8 \your weapon accuracy + nat accuracy\
STRENGTH: 1 \your weapon strength + nat strength\

MTOUGHNESS: 8 \Monster's toughness\
MEVASION: 0 \Monster's evasion\

HITDIE: [d10 at least ACCURACY-MEVASION]

HITS: SPEEDdHITDIE

output HITS named "Chance to Hit"

WOUNDDIE: [d10 at least MTOUGHNESS-STRENGTH]
WOUNDS: HITSdWOUNDDIE

output WOUNDS named "Chance to Wound"
