function: ROLL plus MOD vs DC 
{result: ROLL+MOD>=DC}

ROLL:2d10 DC:{11..20}
loop DC over DC{
output [ROLL plus 0 vs DC] named "[ROLL]+0 vs [DC]"
\output [ROLL plus 1 vs DC] named "[ROLL]+1 vs [DC]"\
output [ROLL plus 2 vs DC] named "[ROLL]+2 vs [DC]"
\output [ROLL plus 3 vs DC] named "[ROLL]+3 vs [DC]"\
output [ROLL plus 4 vs DC] named "[ROLL]+4 vs [DC]"
}
