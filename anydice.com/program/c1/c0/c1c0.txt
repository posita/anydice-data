\ function to determine if an attack hits, misses or crits \
function: hits from ATTACKMOD:n with CRITRANGE:n and CRITMULT:n and CONFIRMMOD:n against AC:n {

\ STRANGE DICE TO USE LATER \
\ Build a miss die (d20 with all 0s) \
MISSDIE: {0:20}

\ Build a hit die (d20 with all 1s) \
HITDIE: {1:20}

\ The number needed to roll to hit \
TARGET: AC - ATTACKMOD  

\ how many numbers on the d20 will be a miss \
MISS: [lowest of 19 and [highest of 0 and TARGET-1]] 

\ not counting a natural 20, and ignoring criticals, how many numbers on the d20 will be a hit \
HIT: 20-MISS

\ Build the critical confirmation dice (d20 with 1s for a normal hit and CRITMOD for a critical) \
CONFIRMDIE: {1:[highest of 0 and MISS - CONFIRMMOD], CRITMOD:[lowest of 20 and HIT + CONFIRMMOD]}

\ Build the threat range, how many ways can you generate a critical threat that are not a natural 20 \
THREATRANGE: [lowest of CRITRANGE and HIT]

\ Remove threatrange from hit chances \
HIT: [highest of 0 and HIT - THREATRANGE]

\ Create the result die - a 400 sided die (d20 x d20) with 0s for a miss, 1s for a normal hit and 2s for a confirmed critical \
result: {MISSDIE:MISS, HITDIE:HIT, CONFIRMDIE:THREATRANGE}
}

\ INPUTS \
ATTACKMOD: +10
CRITRANGE: 3
CRITMOD: 2
CONFIRMMOD: 2
AC: 20

output [hits from ATTACKMOD with CRITRANGE * 2 and CRITMOD and CONFIRMMOD against AC] named "Improved Critical"

output [hits from ATTACKMOD with CRITRANGE  and CRITMOD and CONFIRMMOD against AC] named "Improved Critical"
