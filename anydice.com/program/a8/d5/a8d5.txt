function: test X for Y{ \enter the probability of getting a good item (in integer percent) as x and the number of rooms you'd buy keys for as Y\
 result: Yd(1d100<=X) \the 1d100 portion checks the percentage and returns 1 if you'd get a good item. the Yd runs that operation for for each room and sums the number of good items.\
}
output [test 25 for 5] 
output [test 13 for 5] \this is the point where you have 50/50 odds of getting at least one item.\
\If you look at the at least tab, you can see the odds for getting at least a certain number of good items by buying keys. Since not buying keys only lets you buy 1 item, with some change left over, the odds for getting at least 2 items are the odds of beating key meta and the odds for getting at least 1 item are the odds of breaking even, assuming you always get a good item from saving up for the shop. If you want the real odds of getting a good item from the shop, it would be 1-(the odds of any given item being good^9), since there are 3 shops you'd be able to buy an item from (it takes 3 floors to accrue enough coins from not buying keys to get an item) and each shop should have about 3 items. This doesn't take into account each item coming from a different pool or how re rolls effect things, but it's a decent enough approximation.\
