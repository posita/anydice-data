\This first function is the usual one you want to use for normal attacks that don't have Deadly or Fatal\
function: DIE:n plus MOD:n to hit vs armor class AC:n dealing DMG:n{
TOTAL: DIE+MOD
if ((TOTAL >= AC+10 & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL >= AC)) {result: DMG*2}
else if ((TOTAL >= AC & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL < AC) | (DIE = 1 & TOTAL >= AC+10)) {result: DMG}
else if ((TOTAL >= AC-10 & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL <  AC-10) | (DIE = 1 & TOTAL > AC)) {result: 0}
else {result: 0}}

\This is a secondary function I made kinda quickly to account for Fatal. You'll have to tweak it a bit to work on other sizes of Fatal die, but I have faith in your abilities. The reason for the extra +4 on the Fatal demo is just because this website's a bit finnicky, so just always make sure it's the same as your normal damage mod.\
function: DIE:n plus MOD:n to hit vs armor class AC:n dealing DMG:n fatal DAMAGE:n{
TOTAL: DIE+MOD
if ((TOTAL >= AC+10 & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL >= AC)) {result: DAMAGE*2+1d12}
else if ((TOTAL >= AC & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL < AC) | (DIE = 1 & TOTAL >= AC+10)) {result: DMG}
else if ((TOTAL >= AC-10 & DIE != 1) | (DIE = 20 & TOTAL <  AC-10) | (DIE = 1 & TOTAL > AC)) {result: 0}
else {result: 0}}
