
function: deal DAMAGE:d to enemy with resistance DR:n and threshold DT:n{
   DTMOD: [ammo vs armour 1 0 vs DT]
   result: [highest of (((DAMAGE)*(100-[lowest of DR and 85])/(100))-DTMOD) and DAMAGE/5]
}

function: ammo vs armour MULT:d PEIRCE vs DT:n{

   result: [highest of 0 and (DT*MULT - PEIRCE) ]
}
