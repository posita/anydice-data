\PbtA Ironsworn hack using step die for attributes\
\2d8 are used for difficulty (roll over or equal)\
\0 means fail, 1 means a weak hit or mixed result, 2 means strong hit or full success\
\modifiers involve stepping up or down the attribute die or\
\if you reach a cap (d4 or d20) add or subtract the modifier\

function: ironhack R:s y D:s y DD:s {
WH: R>=D | R>=DD
SH: R>=D & R>=DD

result: WH+SH
}

output [ironhack 1d4-3 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d4 -3"
output [ironhack 1d4-2 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d4 -2"
output [ironhack 1d4-1 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d4 -1"
output [ironhack 1d4 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d4"
output [ironhack 1d6 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d6"
output [ironhack 1d8 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d8"
output [ironhack 1d10 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d10"
output [ironhack 1d12 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d12"
output [ironhack 1d20 y 1d8 y 1d8] named "d20"
output [ironhack 1d20+1 y d8 y d8] named "d20 +1"
output [ironhack 1d20+2 y d8 y d8] named "d20 +2"
output [ironhack 1d20+3 y d8 y d8] named "d20 +3"
