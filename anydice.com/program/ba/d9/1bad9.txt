ENDURANCE: 8
SEEKERS: 1
RA: [highest 2 of SEEKERSd6]
RB: [highest 2 of (SEEKERS+1)d6]
RC: [highest 2 of (SEEKERS+2)d6]
RD: [highest 2 of (SEEKERS+3)d6]
RE: [highest 2 of (SEEKERS+4)d6]
PROBA: RA >= ENDURANCE
PROBB: RB >= ENDURANCE
PROBC: RC >= ENDURANCE
PROBD: RD >= ENDURANCE
PROBE: RE >= ENDURANCE
RUINA: [count SEEKERSd6 in SEEKERSd6]
RUINB: [count SEEKERSd6 in (SEEKERS+1)d6]
RUINC: [count SEEKERSd6 in (SEEKERS+2)d6]
RUIND: [count SEEKERSd6 in (SEEKERS+3)d6]
RUINE: [count SEEKERSd6 in (SEEKERS+4)d6]
A: PROBA * RUINA
B: (!PROBA & PROBB) * (RUINA + RUINB)
C: (!PROBA & !PROBB & PROBC) * (RUINA + RUINB + RUINC)
D: (!PROBA & !PROBB & !PROBC & PROBD) * (RUINA + RUINB + RUINC + RUIND)
E: (!PROBA & !PROBB & !PROBC & !PROBD & PROBE) * (RUINA + RUINB + RUINC + RUIND + RUINE)
output A+B+C+D+E named "Expected Ruin"
output PROBA | PROBB | PROBC | PROBD | PROBE named "Total Success Chance"
