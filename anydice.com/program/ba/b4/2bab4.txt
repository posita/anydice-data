function: compare Q:d and R:d and S:d to W:d and X:d and Y:d for A:s{
T:0
Z:0
T:[count A in R] + [count A in S] + [count A in Q]
Z:[count A in X] + [count A in Y] + [count A in W]
result: T - Z
}

loop N over {1..6}{
output [compare 3d8 and 0d12 and 0d6 to Nd8 and 0d12 and 0d6 for {6..12}] named "3d8 vs [N]d8"
}
