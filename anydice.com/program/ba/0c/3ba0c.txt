DEX: d8
INS: d8
MIG: d10
WIL: d8

DEF: 10
MDEF: 10

function: attack STATONE:n and STATTWO:n acc ACCURACY:n dmg DAMAGE:n vs DEFENSE:n {
    if STATONE + STATTWO + ACCURACY > DEFENSE {
        result: [highest of STATONE and STATTWO] + DAMAGE
    }
    result: 0
}

output [attack MIG and MIG acc 0 dmg 8 vs DEF]
output [attack WIL and MIG acc 0 dmg 12 vs DEF]
