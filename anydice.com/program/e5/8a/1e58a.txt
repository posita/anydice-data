DC: 1
DAMAGE: 1
ACCURACY: 1
ENEMY_ARMOR: 0

\ DC - The Difficulty Check of the attack roll: typically this is the Evasion/E-Defense of your target. \
\ DAMAGE - The roll you make for damage, in dice. This can be 1d3, 1d6 + 4, whatever. \
\ ACCURACY: The number of Accuracy or Difficulty you have to make the check. Use negative numbers to represent Difficulty. e.g. 1 is 1 Acc, -2 is 2 Diff. \
\ ENEMY_ARMOR: The Armor stat of your target. Reduces incoming damage. \

function: roll ROLL:n at difficulty DC:n for DAMAGEROLL:n {
 if ROLL < DC {
  result: 0
 }
 result: DAMAGEROLL
}

output [lowest of [roll 1d20 + [highest 1 of ACCURACYd6] at difficulty DC for DAMAGE - ARMOR] and 1] named "attack fail vs success chance"
output [roll 1d20 + [highest 1 of ACCURACYd6] at difficulty DC for DAMAGE - ARMOR] named "to-hit odds + damage odds"
output DAMAGE named "damage odds only"
