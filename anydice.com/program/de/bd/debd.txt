function: roll DICEPOOL:s at DIF {
 result: (DICEPOOL >= DIF)
}

function: roll DICEPOOL:s at DIF with botches{
 result: (DICEPOOL >= DIF) - (DICEPOOL = 1)
}

DICEPOOL: 5d10
DIF: 6

output [roll DICEPOOL at DIF ] named "roll [DICEPOOL] at dif. [DIF] (no botches)"

output [roll DICEPOOL at DIF with botches] named "roll [DICEPOOL] at dif. [DIF] (botches: -1s are subtracted)"
