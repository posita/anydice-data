\ Damage + Effect spell Balance Calculator \

FD: 1d12                                      \Damage Per Hit\
BS: 0                                         \Strength of applied effect per hit\
H: 1                                          \Estimated number of attempted Hits\

HD: FD+BS                                     \Sum of damage and effect, for MP cost\
SA: (HD*62/100)+(((38*HD)/200))               \Accuracy Calculation for MP cost\
AD: (FD*62/100)+(((38*FD)/200))               \Accuracy Calculation for Damage\
TD: SA*H                                      \Target multiplier for MP cost\
ATD: AD*H                                     \Total damage, summing the damage against all targets attempted to hit and accounting for accuracy\
MPC: TD*4/5                                   \Balanced Mana Point Cost, with damage accounted for accuracy\

output FD named "Hit Damage"
output AD named "Accuracy Hit Damage"
output ATD named "All Target Total Accuracy Hit Damage"
output MPC named "Balanced Mana Point Cost"
