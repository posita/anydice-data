
function: roll L:n levels of health at HD:n and C:n con {
    if L <= 1 {
        result: HD+C
    }
    result: [roll (L-1) levels of health at HD and C con] + 1dHD + C
}

function: average L:n levels of health at HD:n and C:n con {
    if L <= 1 {
        result: HD+C
    }
    result: [average (L-1) levels of health at HD and C con] + HD / 2 + 1 + C
}

output [average 10 levels of health at 6 and 4 con] named "Azazel's health if he took average"

output [roll 10 levels of health at 6 and 4 con] named "Azazel's health if he rolled without fudging the numbers"
