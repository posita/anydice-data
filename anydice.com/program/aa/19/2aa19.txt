HIT: 1d20 <= 13

\To adjust chance to hit, you can change the 13. Increasing by 1 increases chance to hit by 5%, and decreasing by one decreases by 5%. 10=50%\

STUN: 1d20 <= 10

\To adjust the stun rate, same thing as hit rate.\




STUN_B: STUN | STUN

STUN_C: STUN | STUN | STUN

NUM_HITS: HIT + HIT + HIT

function: A:n and B:n and C:n and D:n {
if A = 1{result: B}

if A = 2{result: C}

if A = 3{result: D}

result: 0
}

output NUM_HITS named "Number of hits"

output STUN named "Chance to stun with one hit"

output STUN_B named "Chance to stun with two hits"

output STUN_C named "Chance to stun with three hits"

output [NUM_HITS and STUN and STUN_B and STUN_C] named "Overall chance to stun"
