\Day 1 Jewel Dust Economy\
\Set these three variables and press Calculate\

YOUR_JEWEL_LEVEL: 15
YOUR_JEWEL_DUST: 0
TARGET_LEVEL: 16

\How to interpret results:\
\% is the chance for that specific outcome\
\if # is negative, then that's the dust missing for the upgrade\
\if # is positive, then that's the dust remaining after the upgrade\
\all positive? congrats you're guaranteed on worst scenario\
\all negative? that upgrade is impossible\
\if you want to see your overall chances of having enough dust, set Data to At Least and check % of first positive #\

\Don't edit anything under this\

TEMP_LVL: TARGET_LEVEL
REQUIRED_DUST: 0
loop N over {1..TARGET_LEVEL-YOUR_JEWEL_LEVEL}{
 REQUIRED_DUST: REQUIRED_DUST + 10*2^(TEMP_LVL-1)
 TEMP_LVL: TEMP_LVL-1
}
MISSING_DUST: REQUIRED_DUST-YOUR_JEWEL_DUST
output MISSING_DUST named "missing dust"
